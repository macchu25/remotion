# Sound Design Skill

Guidelines for mixing multi-track audio in Remotion videos.

## Core Rules

1. **Voiceover Hierarchy**:
   - Voiceover must always be the primary audio track (`volume={1.0}`).
   - Pacing: 1.1x to 1.3x speed for shorts; 1.0x for deep masterclasses.

2. **Background Music (BGM) Ducking**:
   - Keep BGM volume low (`volume={0.12 - 0.16}`) so it sits comfortably behind speech.
   - Loop BGM tracks cleanly across long videos.

3. **Impact SFX**:
   - Place pop/click sounds (`pop.wav`) on text pop-ins and typing animations (`volume={0.2 - 0.3}`).
   - Place chime/success sounds (`success.wav`) at key transition frames (`volume={0.6 - 0.8}`).

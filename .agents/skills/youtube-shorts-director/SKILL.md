# YouTube Shorts Director Skill

Guidelines for orchestrating full-length short-form video production pipelines.

## Workflow Pipeline

1. **Scripting & TTS Generation**:
   - Write structured paragraphs with strong 3-second hook.
   - Synthesize Vietnamese AI voiceover and apply 1.25x speed.

2. **Full-Screen 9:16 Composition Architecture**:
   - Set resolution to `1080x1920` (30 FPS).
   - Divide lesson into 15-30 second full-screen `<Sequence>` chapters.

3. **Audio-Visual Synchronization**:
   - Align scene switches with narration beats.
   - Embed CapCut-style kinetic subtitles with active word highlights.
